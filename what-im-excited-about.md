# What I'm excited about

Vibe coding a compiler, of course!

But why? What does that mean?

We're reacting to being given a superpower—sometimes as individuals, sometimes as teams, sometimes as teams competing with other teams. I don't have a business prediction to make about that. I care about what it means for the craft.

I get to have it *all*, my way. My operating system. My language. My keyboard. My machine instructions. My idea of clarity.

So I keep fixing the problems I care about. After enough of them, they start to outline a mold for how the browser, the filesystem, the shell, and everything else should work.

Then I melt the language, and whatever it comes into contact with, into that mold until it fills the crevices of those problems. Then I let it harden.
